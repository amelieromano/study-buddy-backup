# Study Buddy Finder

A first simple version for UCL students.

## The flow

1. A home page with one button, "Find a study buddy at UCL".
2. Clicking it opens a page of students who are free to study: each one has an avatar, their first name and their course.
3. A filter by course, so you can find people on your course.
4. A "Nudge" button on each card to ask if they want to meet up.

## For now

- Fake data only: no login, no database.
- A nudge just changes the button to "Nudged!".
